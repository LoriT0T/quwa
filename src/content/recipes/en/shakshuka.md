ingredients:
  - 4 whole eggs
  - 120 g lean beef mince (5% fat) or 150 g firm tofu
  - 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  - 1 onion, diced
  - 1 red pepper, sliced
  - 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  - 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 1 tsp sweet paprika
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 30 g feta, crumbled
  - Salt, black pepper, fresh parsley
method:
  - Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Soften the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
  - Add the garlic, cumin and paprika. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  - Add the mince and break it up. Cook until no pink remains, about 4 minutes.
  - Pour in the tomatoes, season, and simmer for 6 minutes until it thickens.
  - Make four wells and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook 5–7 minutes for a set white and a soft yolk.
  - Scatter the feta and parsley over the top and serve straight from the pan.

Shakshuka is usually a low-protein breakfast dressed up as a meal. Adding lean mince takes it from
around 18 g of protein per serving to 34 g without changing what it tastes like, and it costs you
four minutes.

If you are cooking for one, halve everything and use a smaller pan — the depth of the tomato base
matters more than the quantity, because a thin base will not hold the eggs.
