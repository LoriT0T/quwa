ingredients:
  - 180 g chicken breast, cut into strips
  - 75 g rice, uncooked (or 200 g pre-cooked)
  - 100 g frozen mixed vegetables
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 1 tsp honey
  - 1 clove garlic, crushed
  - 1 tsp cornflour
  - Spring onion and sesame seeds to finish
method:
  - Start the rice. It is the only thing here with a fixed clock.
  - Toss the chicken strips with the cornflour, salt and pepper.
  - Heat a pan hot, add the sesame oil, and sear the chicken for 4 minutes without moving it too much.
  - Add the garlic and vegetables. Cook 3 minutes.
  - Stir the soy sauce and honey through with a splash of water. It will thicken in under a minute.
  - Serve over the rice with spring onion and sesame.

This is the meal that carries a training block. It is not interesting, and that is the point —
it takes fifteen minutes, it hits your protein target, and you will not get bored of it fast
enough to matter.

To scale it: the chicken sets your protein and the rice sets your carbohydrate. Move them
independently. Adding 25 g of dry rice adds roughly 90 kcal and 20 g of carbohydrate.
