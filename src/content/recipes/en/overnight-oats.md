recipe: overnight-oats
title: "Protein overnight oats"
summary: "Assembled in three minutes the night before, so breakfast is not a decision you have to make."
servings: 1
prepMinutes: 3
cookMinutes: 0
perServing: { kcal: 486, protein: 38, carbs: 54, fat: 12 }
ingredients:
  - 60 g rolled oats
  - 200 g plain Greek yoghurt (0–2% fat)
  - 25 g whey or casein protein powder
  - 150 ml milk of choice
  - 1 tbsp chia seeds
  - 80 g berries
  - Cinnamon, and honey if you want it
method:
  - Stir the protein powder into the milk first, in the jar, until there are no lumps. This is the only step that matters.
  - Add the oats, yoghurt and chia seeds. Stir properly.
  - Fold the berries through, or keep them on top if you prefer them intact.
  - Cover and refrigerate for at least six hours.
  - Loosen with a splash of milk in the morning if it has set too firm.
order: 30
seoDescription: "Protein overnight oats with 38 g of protein per serving — three minutes of assembly, no cooking."
---

Breakfast is the meal people skip when they are busy, and it is the meal where skipping costs the
most protein. Three minutes the night before removes the decision entirely.

The protein powder goes into the liquid first. If you add it to the oats and yoghurt together it
will clump, and no amount of stirring afterwards fixes it.
