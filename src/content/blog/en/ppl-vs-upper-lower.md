The split argument is mostly noise. Weekly volume and consistent progression determine your
results; the split determines how conveniently they fit in your week.

That said, the choice is not arbitrary.

## Days available decides it

**Three days.** Neither. Run full body. Three full-body sessions hit every muscle three times a
week, which beats a split that only reaches each muscle once.

**Four days.** Upper lower. Two upper and two lower sessions give every muscle a frequency of two,
which is where the evidence is most consistent. This is the highest return-per-session option in
this list.

**Five days.** Upper lower with a fifth day assigned to whatever lags. Or a rolling push pull legs
that ignores the calendar week — but rolling splits are administratively annoying and most people
abandon them.

**Six days.** Push pull legs, run twice through. This is what PPL is designed for, and it is the
only configuration where it clearly beats upper lower.

## The mistake almost everyone makes

Running a six-day split on four available days. You end up hitting each muscle once every eight or
nine days, at which point you are training each muscle group roughly 40 times a year instead of
100.

Frequency is not magic on its own, but it is how you distribute weekly volume into sessions you
can actually complete with quality. Twenty sets of chest in one session is not the same stimulus
as ten sets twice.

## Session length matters more than people admit

A four-day upper lower with 70-minute sessions and a six-day PPL with 50-minute sessions can carry
identical weekly volume. The difference is that the six-day version demands you get to the gym six
times, and the four-day version demands you concentrate for longer.

Pick the failure mode you are less likely to hit.

## What actually decides your results

In descending order: whether you turn up, whether your weekly volume is in a sensible range,
whether load or reps go up over a block, whether you sleep and eat enough to recover.

The split is somewhere below all of that. Choose the one you will complete, then stop reading
articles about splits and go and complete it.
