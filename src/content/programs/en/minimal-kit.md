## The first year is the cheapest muscle you will ever build

Beginners respond to almost anything, which is exactly why so much of that first year gets wasted
on programs designed for people ten years further along. This block is not a scaled-down advanced
program. It is written for the situation you are actually in.

## Nineteen movements, taught properly

Every exercise has written technique notes: the setup, the two errors people make, and what it
should feel like when it is right. Video links are there too, but the writing is the part you will
re-read between sets.

## Progression without arithmetic

When you can complete every set at the top of the rep range, with two good reps left in the tank,
you add weight. That is the whole rule. No percentages, no estimated maxes, nothing to calculate
while you are out of breath.
