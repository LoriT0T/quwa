## The problem is decisions, not recipes

You do not lack access to recipes. You lack a short list of things you already know work, that
hit your protein target, that you can cook on a Tuesday when you are tired. That is what this is.

## Macros you can check

Every figure comes from raw ingredient weights and published food composition tables. The method
is on page one. If you think a number is wrong you can verify it, which is not true of most
recipe collections.

## Portions that move

The spreadsheet takes your calorie and protein targets and rescales everything — quantities,
macros and the shopping list. A recipe that fits a 2,800 kcal day and one that fits an 1,800 kcal
day should not be two different recipes.
