## For the point where more effort stopped working

Advanced training is not harder training. It is better-targeted training, because the general
stimulus that worked in year one stopped working somewhere in year three.

This block does two things about that. It counts volume per muscle group so you know what you are
actually doing rather than what you think you are doing. And it gives you two sessions a week
aimed at whatever your audit says is furthest behind.

## Volume as a number

Every session records hard sets per muscle group. The sheet totals them weekly. When your bench
stalls, you can look at whether your weekly chest volume actually went up over the last month, or
whether it only felt like it did.

## The brake matters more than the accelerator

Two fatigue audits are scheduled into the block. Most advanced lifters do not need permission to
push; they need a written rule that tells them when to stop, before the point where a shoulder
makes the decision for them.
