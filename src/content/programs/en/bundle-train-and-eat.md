## Training and food are one decision

A hypertrophy block run on unplanned eating produces a fraction of the result. This bundle puts
the twelve-week program and the recipe collection together, and adds a short document that does
the part neither product does alone: setting your intake for this specific block, and pointing at
the recipes that hit it.
