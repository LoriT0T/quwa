## Six blocks, one price

Bought separately these come to considerably more than the bundle price. The saving is arithmetic,
not a countdown timer — the figure is shown on this page and it does not change if you come back
tomorrow.

## A sequencing guide is included

The most common question about a multi-program purchase is what to run first. The bundle includes
a short guide that maps a route through the six based on your training age, your equipment and
what your last year of training looked like.
