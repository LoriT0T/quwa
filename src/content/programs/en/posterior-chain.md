## Organised by pattern, not by muscle

The usual glute program is a list of glute exercises. This one is organised by hip angle, because
the same muscle behaves differently at 90 degrees of flexion and at full extension, and covering
the range is what produces a complete result.

## The screen comes first

Week one is a hinge screen. It tells you whether to start with a Romanian deadlift, a supported
variation or a bridge pattern, based on what your hips can currently do rather than on what the
program would prefer.

## Upper back is not an afterthought

Two rowing sessions a week are in here for a reason. The back of the body is a chain, and training
the bottom half of it while ignoring the top produces exactly the imbalance the block is meant to fix.
